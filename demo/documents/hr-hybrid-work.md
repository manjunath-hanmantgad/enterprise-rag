id: hr-hybrid-work
title: Hybrid Work Policy
department: Human Resources
allowed_roles: all
---

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week when their manager confirms team coverage. New hires should spend their first two weeks in office for onboarding and security setup. Managers must document recurring exceptions in the HR operations tracker.

The company reimburses approved ergonomic equipment up to 250 USD per employee each calendar year. Requests require a receipt, manager approval, and confirmation that the item supports home office work.
