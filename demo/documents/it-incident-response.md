title: Incident Response Runbook
department: IT Security
allowed_roles: engineer,admin
---

Security incidents must be triaged within fifteen minutes of detection. Engineers classify incidents as severity one when customer data, production credentials, or payment systems may be affected. The incident commander opens a bridge, assigns an evidence owner, and records every decision in the incident log.

Credential exposure requires immediate key rotation, affected service inventory, and a post-incident review within three business days. Public communication is handled only by the executive communications owner.
