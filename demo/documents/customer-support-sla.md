title: Customer Support SLA
department: Customer Operations
allowed_roles: all
---

Priority one support tickets require first response within thirty minutes and executive notification when service interruption affects more than three enterprise customers. Priority two tickets require first response within four business hours.

Support managers review breached service-level agreements every Friday and assign corrective actions to the owning team. Repeated breaches trigger a customer success risk review.
